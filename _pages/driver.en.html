MuskCustoms wide fender flares.

History / About me

From a very young age, I was fascinated by spectacular car driving. I analyzed how cars and drivers reacted to changing conditions on the track. I paid particular attention to vehicle control during a slide. The moment I experienced it myself, I knew I had found my life’s passion.

I began my motorsport career with a rear‑wheel‑drive Lada 2107. After basic repairs, I took part in my first amateur rallies. I saved every penny to drive the way I imagined — pushing the car’s limits further and further. The hard work paid off, and in the same year I managed to win at the provincial level (competing against crews with almost twice the power) in 2007.

I quickly realized that something was missing in this form of motorsport. I loved driving in slides, but racing against the clock didn’t allow for direct competition. Fortunately, at the beginning of the new millennium, drifting started becoming popular in Poland. The first drifting competitions appeared, which motivated me to take part in them.
