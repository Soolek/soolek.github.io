Drifting since 2008. Regional champion 2024 (Driftingowe Mistrzostwa Podbeskidzia) and winner of Drift Show Podkarpacie Dynów 2024. Show driver at Warsaw Motor Show, Tuning Show Expo and MotoOrkiestra. Car: BMW E46, 519 HP, homologated roll cage.

Car specification

Body Lightened BMW E46 sedan with an expanded, homologated safety cage.
Lightweight doors, hood, and trunk lid by Mikinka Project.
MuskCustoms wide fender flares.
HM Sports Aero body kit.
Engine M54B30, forged Wiseco pistons and Turboworks rods
Power 519 HP, 652 Nm
Turbo Pulsar PSR 3076 Gen2
ECU EMU Black
Gearbox BMW M57 (GS5-39DZ), lightweight single‑mass flywheel, Poweride twin‑disc clutch, Sachs Performance pressure plate
Drivetrain Type 210 differential, 3.64 ratio
Suspension ISC Drift
Steering angle kit Full CNC71 angle kit (control arms, angle adapters), fully adjustable rear arms
Fuel system Motorsport fuel system based on a Bosch 044 pump with an overflow swirl pot
Handbrake Hydraulic handbrake with separate brake calipers.

History / About me

Cars fascinated me as a child. I watched how drivers handled a sliding car and wanted to feel it myself. The first time I did, I knew this was my sport.

I started with a rear-wheel-drive Lada 2107 in amateur rallies. I saved every złoty for the car and pushed it further at every event. In 2007 I won at provincial level against crews with almost twice my power.

Rallying against the clock was not enough. I wanted to drive sideways and compete head to head. When drifting arrived in Poland I entered my first competition in 2008 and never stopped.
