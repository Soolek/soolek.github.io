BENEFITS AND SERVICES FOR THE PARTNER

What does your business gain?

We don't stop at a sticker on the car. We create a dedicated promotional platform for your products, customers and employees:

  • Drift Taxi & Co-Driving (Unique B2B Events):

    I provide a professionally prepared drift car together with full coaching support. It is a perfect tool for building relationships with your key (VIP) clients, or a unique bonus / team-building experience for your employees.

  • Exposure and a Mobile Trade Show Stand:

    I guarantee the presence of the branded race car with its full setup at your industry trade shows, Open Days or corporate events. A race car is a proven magnet that draws potential customers to the stand.

  • R&D Test Platform:

    I make the race car and telemetry support available as a real test platform (R&D) for your innovative products, components or software. You get hard data from tests in extreme conditions and an invaluable proof of quality (Proof of Concept) that you can use in your brand's marketing.

  • Traditional Brand Exposure:

    A prominent spot on the race suit, the car (paddock + track), the team's outfits and in photo / video materials.
