Where you will be seen

A typical season is more than ten events between April and November: trade fairs, drift shows and competitions across Poland.

Trade fairs and shows

  • Warsaw Motor Show: Poland's largest motoring fair, over 100,000 visitors.
  • Tuning Show Expo Kraków: the key event in southern Poland, over 14,000 visitors.
  • MotoOrkiestra Kraków: a charity motoring event with drift shows and thousands of fans each year.

Competitions

  • Drift Show Podkarpacie, Driftingowe Mistrzostwa Podbeskidzia, Baltic Drift Cup and regional rounds, mostly in southern Poland.
  • On request: Drift Open or PDS Polish Drift Show rounds, chosen to match your markets.
